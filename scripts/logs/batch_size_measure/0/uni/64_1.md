# Batch vs serial update by batch size

Time to insert / delete the first k entries of one batch into a tree built
from 1e9 uniform entries. Leaf 64, 224 threads, mean of 3 runs (one warm-up
first), tree rebuilt before every run. Raw seconds: `64_1.csv`. Script:
`scripts/batch_size_measure.sh`.

| k | insert: batch | insert: serial | delete: batch | delete: serial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 17 µs | **2.6 µs** | 24 µs | **3.2 µs** |
| 10 | 70 µs | **25 µs** | 43 µs | **24 µs** |
| 100 | **95 µs** | 214 µs | **61 µs** | 223 µs |
| 1,000 | **0.32 ms** | 1.9 ms | **0.17 ms** | 2.0 ms |
| 10,000 | **1.1 ms** | 17 ms | **0.58 ms** | 17 ms |
| 100,000 | **4.9 ms** | 146 ms | **2.2 ms** | 148 ms |
| 1 million | **19 ms** | 1.36 s | **13 ms** | 1.41 s |
| 10 million | **75 ms** | 13.0 s | **81 ms** | 13.6 s |
| 100 million | **0.19 s** | 168 s | **0.21 s** | 137 s |

Bold: faster.

- Serial is faster up to 10 entries: about 7× at k = 1, 2–3× at k = 10.
- Batch is faster from 100 entries on: 660–870× at 100 million. Serial
  costs a steady 1.3–1.7 µs per entry.
